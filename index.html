Concrete outcomes, not just dashboards

The goal is simple: reduce avoidable strain, improve staffing decisions, and give leaders the visibility to act before a people problem turns into a business problem.

🛡️

Reduced overload

Identify teams and roles carrying unsustainable pressure so work can be redistributed before burnout, delays, and disengagement compound.

⏱️

Earlier intervention

Flag higher-risk employees and emerging stress patterns sooner, giving HR and managers time to respond while options are still available.

🧭

Better staffing visibility

Connect hiring demand, workload pressure, and team structure into one view so leaders can plan recruitment with clearer business context.

Demo story

Marketing team over capacity, high-risk employees flagged, manager scorecard recommends rebalancing

A department head opens People Pulse and immediately sees the Marketing team running above sustainable capacity. Two employees are flagged as high-risk based on combined workload and absence-related indicators, while a manager scorecard recommends rebalancing campaign work and reviewing headcount coverage.

  • Pressure detected: Workload trend spikes beyond target capacity for the team.
  • Risk surfaced: High-risk employees are highlighted for earlier support and follow-up.
  • Action recommended: The manager scorecard suggests redistributing work and escalating staffing need.
